Why automate

Less copy-paste. More control.

Manual finance reporting often means repeated exports, formulas, follow-ups, and version confusion. Automation creates a repeatable data flow, while dashboards turn that structured data into information management can actually review and act on.

Before

Reporting depends on manual effort

  • Repeated Tally exports
  • Copy-paste into multiple sheets
  • Formula errors and stale versions
  • Delayed receivable/payable visibility
  • Management asks for the same report again
→Automate
After

One structured finance data flow

  • Scheduled or on-demand sync
  • Standardized registers and masters
  • Validation and exception logs
  • Always-ready dashboards
  • Faster drill-down by customer, month or ledger
